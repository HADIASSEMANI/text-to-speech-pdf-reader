02/04

Features

PDF, EPUB, and pasted text

Open a PDF or EPUB or paste a passage directly; EPUB headings, lists, superscripts, subscripts, MathML equations, and printed page numbers keep their structure.

Synchronized highlighting & click-to-read

Word and line highlighting follows the audio in real time, and clicking any passage starts reading right from that spot.

Saved reading progress to the second

Your exact position is anchored in a local SQLite database and resumes where you left off across restarts and changes to narration settings.

Local Kokoro voices at your pace

Select a Kokoro-82M voice and adjust playback speed for your book while upcoming segments preload and cache in the background.

Chapter, page, and 15-second navigation

Skip 15 seconds forward or backward seamlessly across segment boundaries, or jump straight to any chapter or printed page.

Clean layout filtering

Automatically skips running headers, footers, and page numbers, with optional toggles to include footnotes, captions, and sidebars.

03/04

Privacy

Your books stay on your computer.

  • Kokoro generates speech locally on your computer.
  • No account. No telemetry, analytics, or cloud services.
  • Your books and pasted text are never uploaded.
  • Your library, reading position, and generated audio cache are stored locally on your disk, with a Clear cache button in Settings whenever you want to free space.

The initial download needs a connection. The app downloads Kokoro’s configuration, model weights, and selected voice files from Hugging Face (hexgrad/Kokoro-82M) when they aren’t already cached. After that, speech generation runs completely offline.

What is stored, and where?

The library and reading progress anchors live in reader.db; generated WAV audio and JSON timing cues live in cache/; pasted texts live in pasted-texts/. The audio cache has a configurable size limit and can be cleared on demand in Settings while keeping your library and reading positions.

Windows
%LOCALAPPDATA%\BookTTSReader (installer defaults to %LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\BookTTSReader)
macOS
~/Library/Application Support/BookTTSReader
Linux
$XDG_DATA_HOME/BookTTSReader (or ~/.local/share/BookTTSReader)

Model and voice downloads are stored in the Hugging Face cache, normally ~/.cache/huggingface/hub (or HF_HOME / HUGGINGFACE_HUB_CACHE). Preferences use Qt’s settings storage under BookTTS / BookTTSReader.

Running from source clears generated audio on exit by default. Set BOOKTTS_KEEP_CACHE=1 to keep it across development runs.

Setup.exe

Run the installer (BookTTSReader-Setup.exe). It installs into %LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\BookTTSReader for your user without administrator privileges.

Clone the repository, create a Python 3.12 virtual environment, install requirements.txt, and run python main.py.
